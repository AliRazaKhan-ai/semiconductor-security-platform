Correction. Earlier project material attributed the hardware stage to Yosys
synthesising two netlists, and stated that Yosys alone took about 21 seconds.
That was inferred from the stage total and never measured. Yosys takes 160ms.
The stage is Verilator building a C++ model with make and gcc.

Commit ab522b0 states the Yosys adapter is constructed per scan. It is not: it
is built once in app/factory.py:157, and the reference cache hits (0.19s cold,
0.09s warm). The cache is correct but saves about 0.1s.

The pipeline records no sub-stage timing. This split exists only because it was
instrumented for this measurement.
