READING THE NUMBERS
  warm_min is the per-stage cost once the process is running. AI_ANALYSIS's
  maximum is dominated by TensorFlow import and model load, paid once per
  process, not per chip.
  HARDWARE_SECURITY is Yosys synthesising two netlists for the reference
  differential, Verilator compiling and executing the testbench, OpenTitan
  attestation, ChipWhisperer trace analysis, SBOM generation and digital-twin
  matching. Six sub-stages across two external toolchains.
  The reference netlist is identical for every chip and is re-synthesised each
  time. Caching it is the largest single available saving.
